Add an opt-in `backToTop` prop to the Footer: a round, light-blue `arrow-up`
button with a dark-blue arrow at the inline end of the footer's navigation row
that scrolls the page back to the top. The accessible label is authored in the
CMS and passed through as the prop; without a label the button does not render.
